iSuite AI Solution Partner Program la, oru Partner iSuite AI-ku kondu vandhu sera client-ku commission kudukkuthu: **Direct Sale-ku 25%** and **Referral-ku 15%**, first payment la and ovvoru renewal la yum.

## 1. Arimugam

Indha program iSuite AI, Madurai, Tamil Nadu nadathuthu. Indha rules ellaa Solution Partner-ku applicable, and Solution Partner Agreement oda paagam dhaan.

## 3. Yaar serlaam & join panradhu (Eligibility and joining)

Join panradhu free. 18 vayasu mela irukkura yaaraavadhu, illa India la register aana business, apply pannalaam.

1. Apply pannuravar oru partner application-ah name, contact details and business details (irundha) oda submit pannanum.
2. Apply pannuravar KYC complete pannanum: PAN, avanga per la illa avanga business per la oru bank account, and GST-registered-nna GSTIN.
3. Apply pannuravar Solution Partner Agreement and indha rules-ah accept pannanum.
4. iSuite AI application-ah paathu approve illa decline pannum. Karanam sollaama kooda iSuite AI decline pannalaam.
5. Approve aana udaney, Partner-ku oru partner login kedaikkum, and velai aarambikkalaam.

## 4. Role-um Responsibility-um (Roles and responsibilities)

Partner Client-ah kondu vandhu convince pannanum. Adhukku apparam ellaam iSuite AI paathukkum.

| Partner | iSuite AI |
| --- | --- |
| iSuite AI-ku porutham aana business-ah kandupidichu, product-ah honest-aa explain pannanum | Client-ah contact pannum, thevai padra edathula demo kudukkum |
| Client-ah Referral Link, Lead Registration illa Direct Sale moolama kondu varanum | Account, WhatsApp number, Meta approval and AI rules ellaam set up pannum |
| iSuite AI publish pannina correct plan details and prices-ah share pannanum | Ellaa payment-ah yum collect pannum, GST invoice kudukkum |
| Client information-ah confidential-aa vechukkanum | Client-ku ellaa support, training and renewals kudukkum |

Partner oru independent contractor. Partner iSuite AI oda employee, agent illa legal representative kidaiyaadhu, and iSuite AI sarbaa contract sign panna illa promise panna mudiyaadhu.

## 5. Edhukku earn pannalaam & commission rates (Ways to earn and commission rates)

Partner rendu sale method moolama earn pannalaam. Lead Registration oru prospect-ah protect pannum, aana thaniyaa commission edhuvum kudukkaadhu.

| Method | Eppadi work aaguthu | Plan Price mela Commission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct Sale | Partner Client-ah convince panni, plan-ah select panni, official iSuite AI payment link anuppuvaar | **25%** |
| Referral | Client Partner oda Referral Link moolama thaaney sign up panni vaanguvaanga | **15%** |
| Lead Registration | Partner interest irukkura oru prospect-ah register panni claim pannuvaar. Appuram antha prospect Direct Sale illa Referral moolama vaangumbodhu, antha method oda rate apply aagum | Thaniyaa commission illa |

- **Renewals:** Ovvoru Renewal la yum adhey rate dhaan kudukkum, eppovarai Client renew pannuraangalo and Partner program la irukkaaro adhuvarai.
- **Upgrades:** Oru Client periya plan-ku maarumbodhu, antha upgrade-ku Client katta amount mela adhey rate la commission kudukkum.
- **Free trial:** Ovvoru Direct Sale payment link-um plan oda free trial la dhaan start aagum. Trial mudinja apparam Client oda first payment vandhaa dhaan commission earn aagum.
- **Basis:** Commission-ah Plan Price mela, GST thaviri, Partner discount (section 8) kazhichu, iSuite AI real-aa vaangitta amount mela dhaan calculate pannum.

> **Example:** Oru clinic Clinic Growth (₹22,999 + GST) ah Direct Sale moolama vaanguthu. Partner 25% × ₹22,999 = ₹5,750 earn pannuvaar, and ovvoru varsha Renewal la yum thirumba ₹5,750.

## 6. Attribution rules (Yaaru-ku commission-nu mudivu pannra rules)

Ovvoru Client-um oru commission dhaan kudukkum, antha commission oru Partner-ku dhaan.

- **Referral Link validity:** Partner oda Referral Link-ah first time click panni, 90 days la vaangura Client antha Partner-ku credit aaguvaanga.
- **Lead Registration lock:** Register pannina oru lead antha Partner-ku 60 days lock aagi irukkum. Antha time la vera yaaru Partner-um adhey lead-ah register illa claim panna mudiyaadhu. 60 days la lead vaangalena, lock mudinjudum, and lead free aagidum.
- **One commission per Client:** Adhey Client oru Partner oda oru method-ku mela moolama iSuite AI-ah adanjaa, oru commission dhaan kudukkum, sale-ah complete pannina method oda rate la.
- **Two Partners, one Client:** Valid Lead Registration lock-ku dhaan priority. Lock illaama irundhaa, Client last-aa use pannina link dhaan mudivu pannum: yaaru Partner oda Referral Link illa payment link purchase-ah complete pannithaa, avanga dhaan credit, munnadi vera Partner oda link click aana irundhaalum.
- **Registration check:** iSuite AI ovvoru Lead Registration-ah yum duplicate-ku check pannum. Yaaru-ku credit-nu iSuite AI oda records dhaan mudivu pannum.

> **Example:** Client day 1 la Partner A oda link click pannuvaanga, aana day 87 la Partner B oda link moolama vaanguvaanga. Partner B-ku dhaan credit.

## 7. Commission kedaikkaadha sales (Sales not eligible for commission)

Idhu ellaam la commission kudukkaadhu:

- Partner oda referral, registration illa payment link-ku munnaadiye Client already iSuite AI customer-aa irundhaa.
- Partner register pannradhuku munnaadiye Client iSuite AI oda sontha sales pipeline la irundhaa.
- Partner avanga sonta business-ku, illa avanga own panra, nadathura illa velai paakkura oru business-ku iSuite AI vaangina (self-referral). Example: oru gym sonta Partner, antha gym-ku vaangina Gym plan-ku commission kedaikkaadhu.
- Sale indha rules-ah follow pannaama irundhaa, example lead-ah register pannaama irundhaa illa official payment link thaviri vera edathula payment vaangi irundhaa.
- Poi claim, fraud illa section 10 breach moolama sale pannina.

## 8. Pricing, discounts & payments

iSuite AI dhaan ellaa plan price-ah yum set pannum. Partner publish aana price la dhaan vikkanum, Direct Sale la Partner discount dhaan exception.

- Partner iSuite AI publish pannina plan-ah and price-ah mattum dhaan quote pannanum.
- **Partner discount (Direct Sale mattum):** Oru Direct Sale la, Partner Client-ku Plan Price oda 25% varai eppadi venaalum discount kudukkalaam. Antha discount Partner oda sonta 25% commission la irundhu dhaan poogum. First payment la and edhaavadhu Renewal la kudukkalaam, ovvoru thadava antha payment oda commission la irundhu.
- **Referral:** Referral sales la discount edhuvum illa. Client publish aana price-ah dhaan kattanum.
- Ovvoru payment-um direct-aa official iSuite AI payment link moolama iSuite AI-ku dhaan poogum. Partner Client kitta irundhu cash, UPI, bank transfer illa vera edhavadhu vazhiyila money collect panna mudiyaadhu.
- iSuite AI oda terms, refund policy and privacy policy ovvoru Client-ku yum apply aagum.
- Oru plan period start aana apparam payment refund aagaadhu. Rare case la, iSuite AI oda refund policy refund allow pannina madiri (double charge illa plan activate aagaama irundhaa), refund aana amount mela commission kudukkaadhu.

> **Example:** Plan Price ₹22,999. Partner ₹2,000 discount kudukkuraar. Client ₹20,999 + GST kattuvaanga, and Partner ₹5,750 − ₹2,000 = ₹3,750 earn pannuvaar.

## 9. Commission payout & taxes

Commission maasathuku oru thadava, 5th thaedhi kudukkum, antha thaedhiku ulla iSuite AI account-ku payment vandhurukkura ellaa Qualified Sales-ku yum.

- **Payout date:** Ovvoru maasamum 5th thaedhi. 5th bank holiday-nna, adutha working day la payout pannum.
- **Method:** KYC la kudutha account-ku bank transfer.
- **TDS:** India income-tax law kekkura edathula mattum iSuite AI TDS pudikkum, antha time la law set panra rate la. Pudicha TDS Partner oda PAN-ku deposit aagum and payout statement la kaattum. Partner adhai avanga income-tax return la claim pannalaam.
- **PAN:** Ovvoru Partner-ku yum PAN mandatory, enna-na PAN kudukkaama irundhaa law romba periya TDS rate kekkuthu. Valid PAN kudukkum varai iSuite AI payout release pannaadhu.
- **GST:** GST-registered Partner commission-ku oru GST invoice anuppanum, and iSuite AI adhuku mela 18% GST katti kudukkum. GST-registered illaadha Partner GST illaama commission vaanguvaanga.
- **Statement:** Ovvoru payout-um oru statement oda varum, adhula ovvoru sale, earn aana commission, pudicha TDS and katti amount ellaam kaattum.

## 10. Partner code of conduct (Partner nadandhukka vendiya mura)

Partner Client munnaadi iSuite AI-ah represent pannuraar, so honest-aa nadandhukkanum.

- **No false claims:** Product guarantee pannaadha result-ah promise panna koodaadhu, madiri fixed number of sales, leads illa bookings, illa product panna mudiyaadha claims.
- **No spam:** Bulk-aa ketkaama WhatsApp message, SMS, email illa call anuppa koodaadhu. Contact list vaanga koodaadhu. WhatsApp misuse pannaa Meta number-ah block pannidum.
- **Brand use:** iSuite AI name, logo and material-ah iSuite AI kudutha madiri mattum dhaan use pannanum. Official iSuite AI channel madiri therira website, ads, social page illa domain name create panna koodaadhu.
- **Paid ads:** Written approval illaama Google, Meta illa vera ads la "iSuite" illa "iSuite AI" word-ku bid panna koodaadhu.
- **Confidentiality:** Client details, Client-ku kudutha prices and iSuite AI oda business information-ah confidential-aa vechukkanum. Client personal data-ah, Client-ah iSuite AI-ku kondu varadhuku mattum, India oda data protection law padi use pannanum.
- **Honest identity:** Ungala oru iSuite AI Solution Partner-nu introduce pan'nunga, iSuite AI employee-nu illa.

## 11. Termination (Program la irundhu vilagardhu)

Rendu paekkamum 30 days written notice kudthu, email illa WhatsApp la, program la irundhu vilagalaam.

- **Immediate termination:** Fraud, false claims, spam, Client money collect panradhu, brand misuse illa indha rules oda edhaavadhu serious breach-ku iSuite AI udaney partnership-ah mudichidum.
- **Earned commission:** Oru Partner vilagumbodhu, vilagura thaedhiku munnaadi Client katti ellaa sale-ku yum commission adutha payout date la katti kudukkum. Fraud illa serious breach-ku Partner-ah remove pannumbodhu mattum antha wrongful sales-ku commission kudukkaadhu.
- **Renewals after exit:** Partnership mudinja udaney Renewal commission nindrudum.
- **Clients stay:** Partner vilaginaalum Client iSuite AI oda customer-aa dhaan irupaanga. iSuite AI avangala thodarndhu service pannum.
