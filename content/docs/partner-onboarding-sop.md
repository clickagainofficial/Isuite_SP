**Internal document. For the iSuite AI team only. Do not share with partners.**

This SOP is the team's side of the **Getting Started** guide. Follow the steps in order for every new Solution Partner. Do not create a partner login until every item in the activation checklist is done.

## At a glance

| Step | What the team does | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Receive | Record the application | Applicant is in the partner list as "Applied" |
| 2. Review | Check eligibility and call the applicant | Approved to continue, or declined |
| 3. KYC | Collect and verify documents | All documents verified |
| 4. Agreement | Send, collect and countersign the Agreement | Both signed copies filed |
| 5. Account | Create the partner login and links | Partner has logged in |
| 6. Welcome | Share material and the ground rules | Partner confirms they are ready |

## Step 1: Receive the application

Applications come in on WhatsApp ("PARTNER" to +91 82209 47112) or through the partner application form.

Record these details for every applicant:

- Business name and the name of the person applying
- Mobile and WhatsApp number, email, city
- How they plan to find clients
- Date the application was received

## Step 2: Review the application

Reply to every applicant within **2 working days**.

1. Check eligibility: the applicant must be a **business registered in India**.
2. Check that the business is not already a partner and has not been removed from the program before.
3. Call the applicant for a short chat: what they do, who their clients are, how they plan to sell.
4. Decide: continue to KYC, or decline.

iSuite AI may decline an application without giving a reason. When declining, send a short, polite message and record the decision.

## Step 3: Collect and verify KYC

| Document | Needed from | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PAN (self-attested copy) | Every partner | Name matches the business or its owner. Number is valid. |
| Cancelled cheque or bank passbook front page | Every partner | Account is in the partner's own name or the business's name. Account number and IFSC are readable. |
| GST certificate | Only GST-registered partners | GSTIN is active and the name matches. |
| Proof of authority to sign | Firms, LLPs and companies | The person signing is allowed to sign for the business. |

> **No PAN, no activation.** Commission cannot be paid without a valid PAN, so do not move to Step 4 until it is verified.

If anything is missing or unclear, tell the applicant exactly what is needed.

## Step 4: Sign the Partner Agreement

1. Fill **Schedule A** of the Solution Partner Agreement with the verified KYC details.
2. Send the Agreement and the Solution Partner Program Rules to the applicant.
3. Collect the signed copy: every page, the partner's signature, and **2 witnesses**.
4. Check that the details in Schedule A match the KYC documents.
5. Sign for iSuite AI and send the countersigned copy to the partner.
6. File both signed copies with the KYC documents.

## Step 5: Create the partner account

1. Create the partner login.
2. Enter the bank account, PAN and GSTIN exactly as verified in KYC.
3. Check that the partner's referral link works.
4. Send the login to the partner on WhatsApp and email.
5. Ask the partner to log in and confirm the bank details on their Profile page.

## Step 6: Welcome the partner

Share:

- The **Getting Started** guide and the **Partner FAQ**
- The **Partner Brochure** and other sales material
- The current plans and published prices

Remind the partner of the three rules that matter most:

1. **Never collect money from a client.** Every payment goes through the official iSuite AI payment link.
2. **Never promise results** the product does not guarantee.
3. **No spam** and no bought contact lists.

## Activation checklist

- [ ] Application recorded and reviewed
- [ ] Applicant is a business registered in India
- [ ] PAN verified
- [ ] Bank account proof verified
- [ ] GST certificate verified (if GST-registered)
- [ ] Proof of authority received (firm, LLP or company)
- [ ] Agreement signed by the partner with 2 witnesses
- [ ] Agreement countersigned by iSuite AI and sent back
- [ ] Partner login created and tested
- [ ] Partner has confirmed their bank details

## Records to keep

Keep these for every partner, for as long as they are a partner and after they leave:

- The application and the review decision
- KYC documents
- Both signed copies of the Agreement
- The date the account was activated
- Any later change to KYC details. Partners must report changes within 7 days.
