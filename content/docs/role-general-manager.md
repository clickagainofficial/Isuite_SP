The General Manager (GM) leads the iSuite AI team and owns the 90-day targets. The GM also leads Marketing directly: the Marketing Executive does the day-to-day work, and the GM sets the plan and approves what goes out.

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Team | Admin & HR, Training Head, Marketing, Partner Relations, Customer Support |
| Main goal | 30 new SPs signed and 25 direct paying customers by 31 March 2027 |

## Responsibilities

### Growth

- Plan new iSuite AI software features from what clients, partners and the team report.
- Help Partner Relations onboard Solution Partners, especially large or well-connected partners.
- Meet new people who can become partners or bring partners: CAs, Tally resellers, agencies, industry suppliers and business associations.

### Marketing

- Leads the Marketing Executive directly. See the [Marketing](/docs/role-marketing/) card for who does what.
- Sets the marketing plan and the Meta ad budget for both funnels.
- Approves every new ad, the week's videos, the PR article, SP demo videos, customer guides and WhatsApp or email campaigns before they go out.
- On Friday, decides where the ad money moves next week.

### Lead the team

- Set the monthly targets on the first working day of the month.
- Run Monday planning and the Friday review.
- Give the behaviour score for the Top Performer of the month.

### Final approvals

- Hiring and every HR letter.
- Every new Solution Partner, and the Partner Agreement signature for iSuite AI.
- Conflicts when two partners claim the same client.
- The monthly SP payout list, by the working day before the 5th.
- Every expense and the Meta ad budget.
