Admin & HR keeps the team running: hiring, HR letters, the morning meeting, the KPI board, team events, expenses and accounts, and the monthly SP payout list.

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Reports to | General Manager |
| Works most with | Everyone |
| Trained by | Training Head |

## Responsibilities

### Hiring and HR

- Post jobs, screen candidates and set up interviews with the GM. All 5 roles hired by 30 November 2026.
- Issue every HR letter from the [HR Letters](/docs/hr-letters/) templates: offer, appointment, confidentiality agreement, salary revision, warning, experience and relieving. The GM signs.
- Keep a file for each employee: letters, ID proof, bank details and the signed confidentiality agreement.
- Keep the attendance and leave record.

### Morning meeting and team culture

- Run the 10:30 AM morning meeting every working day: pledge, yesterday's numbers, today's target. 15 minutes.
- Plan one team event a month for ₹8,000: turf games, a movie or games. Usually the 3rd Saturday. In January, the office Pongal celebration on 14 January.
- Work out the Top Performer score and announce the winner on the last working day.

### KPI board

- Collect everyone's 6:00 PM update and update the daily KPI board (see [90-Day Targets](/docs/team-targets/)).
- On Friday, tell the GM who is below target.

### Accounts and expenses

- Record every expense the same day, with the bill, and get the GM's approval.
- Keep the list of paid tools with cost and renewal date.
- Compare spend with the [Monthly Budget](/docs/team-budget/) with the GM on the last working day.

### SP payouts and partner checks

- Prepare the monthly SP payout list using the **Monthly Payout SOP**. Partner Relations confirms which partner each sale belongs to. The GM approves before the 5th. Pay on the 5th.
- Verify PAN, bank and GST details of new partners (Partner Onboarding SOP).

## December

- Get every HR letter ready and issue appointment letters to everyone.
- Set up employee files, the attendance record and the KPI board sheet.
- Prepare the holiday list for the GM to confirm and plan the December team event (19 December).
