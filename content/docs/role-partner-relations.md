Partner Relations looks after every Solution Partner (SP): calls new partner leads, runs onboarding, trains partners and answers all their questions. **Job title: Partner Relationship Executive.**

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Reports to | General Manager |
| Works most with | General Manager, Admin & HR |
| Trained by | Training Head |

**Simple rule:** if it is about a Solution Partner, it is Partner Relations' work. When a partner's client pays, the client's setup and support move to Customer Support.

## Responsibilities

### New partner leads

- Call every SP lead from Meta ads **within 1 hour** during working hours.
- Explain the program honestly using the Program Rules and the Partner Brochure. Send documents as PDFs, never the site link.

### Onboarding

- Run the **Partner Onboarding SOP** for every applicant: review, KYC, Agreement, account, welcome.
- Onboard **in person** at the Madurai office or fully **online**, whichever the applicant prefers. Online: video call, KYC photos with the original PAN card seen on video, and the signed Agreement as a scan or photo of every page.
- Reply to every applicant within 2 working days.
- The GM approves each new partner and signs the Agreement for iSuite AI. Admin & HR verifies PAN, bank and GST details.

### Getting partners to their first sale

- Train every new partner: the product, the pitch, and how to send referral and payment links. Use Marketing's demo videos.
- Run the **SP webinar every Wednesday at 4:00 PM**.
- Follow up with each new partner every week until their first sale.

### Partner questions

- Answer every partner question the same working day: referral links, lead registration, payment links, commission and payouts.
- When two partners claim the same client, collect the facts and send them to the GM to decide.
- For the monthly payout, confirm which partner each sale belongs to (Monthly Payout SOP).

### Rules

- No live sales demos for partners' clients (Program Rules). Send the demo videos and pitch material instead.
- Make sure no partner ever collects money from a client.
