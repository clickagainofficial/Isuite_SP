The Training Head gives **result-oriented training**: every session ends with a test, and success means the trainee hits their role target, not just attending.

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Reports to | General Manager |
| Trains | Admin & HR, Marketing, Partner Relations, Customer Support |
| Does not train | Solution Partners (Partner Relations trains them) |

## December training month

| Week | Dates | Focus | Test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1–5 Dec | iSuite AI product: all 7 apps, WhatsApp AI, plans and prices | Product test, 80% or more |
| 2 | 7–12 Dec | Role skills: SOPs, scripts and tools for each role | Role test, 80% or more |
| 3 | 14–19 Dec | Practice: mock calls, mock setups, mock payout list | 20 mock calls or tasks per person |
| 4 | 21–31 Dec | Live practice with test-ad leads, final tests | Final test, best trainee on 31 Dec |

A weekly training test runs every Friday at 5:00 PM in December.

## What each role must be able to do

| Role | Must be able to | How we check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admin & HR | Issue HR letters, update the KPI board, prepare a payout list, record expenses | Mock payout list with zero errors, sample letters |
| Marketing | Explain each product in a video, set up a Meta ad, read cost per lead | 3 sample videos the GM approves, 1 test ad set up right |
| Partner Relations | Explain the program, run onboarding, answer partner questions | 20 mock partner calls, onboarding SOP walk-through |
| Customer Support | Set up a client, follow the call schedule, handle doubts | 20 mock calls, 3 mock setups done right |

## After December

- **New hire:** the same 2-week plan (weeks 1 and 2). The new hire works alone by the end of week 2.
- **Refresher:** anyone below 70% of their monthly target gets refresher training the next week, with a new test.
- **Product updates:** a short session for the team whenever a new feature ships.

## KPIs

| KPI | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Product test | Everyone scores 80% or more |
| Ramp-up | A new hire works alone within 2 weeks |
| Result | Trainees reach their role targets |
| Refresher | Within 1 week for anyone below 70% of target |
