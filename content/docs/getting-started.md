Most partners go from application to their first client in about a week. Follow these 7 steps in order. Each step tells you what to do, what you need and what happens next.

## Step 1: Apply

Send your application to the iSuite AI partner team.

- **How:** WhatsApp "PARTNER" to +91 82209 47112, or fill the partner application form.
- **What to share:** Your name (or business name), mobile number, email, city, and how you plan to find clients.
- **Cost:** Free. There is no joining fee.

**What happens next:** Our partner team reviews your application, usually within 2 working days, and may call you for a short chat.

## Step 2: Complete your KYC

KYC lets us verify you and pay your commission to the right account.

| Document | Who needs it |
| --- | --- |
| PAN card (self-attested copy) | Everyone |
| Cancelled cheque or bank passbook front page | Everyone |
| GST certificate | Only if you are GST-registered |
| Proof that you can sign for the business (board resolution, partnership letter or similar) | Only if you join as a firm, LLP or company |

> **PAN is compulsory.** Commission cannot be paid without a valid PAN.

**What happens next:** We verify your documents and tell you if anything is missing.

## Step 3: Sign the Partner Agreement

1. We send you the **Solution Partner Agreement** with your details filled in Schedule A.
2. Read it, along with the **Solution Partner Program Rules**. Ask us if anything is unclear.
3. Sign the agreement, add 2 witnesses, and send the signed copy back to us (scan or photo of every page).
4. We sign our copy and send it to you.

**What happens next:** Your partner account is approved and becomes active.

## Step 4: Log in to your partner dashboard

You receive your partner login on WhatsApp and email. In the dashboard you can:

- Copy your **referral links**
- **Register leads** to lock interested businesses to you
- Create **payment links** for Direct Sales
- Track your **clients, commission and payouts**
- Download **sales material** like brochures and message templates

> **Check your bank details** on the Profile page once you log in. Payouts go only to the verified account.

## Step 5: Find your first prospects

Start with people who already know and trust you.

- **Make a list of 20 businesses** you know personally: clinics, gyms, salons, coaching centres, turfs, builders or service companies.
- **Look for the signs:** they reply to customers late, manage bookings on paper or WhatsApp, chase payments manually, or miss renewals.
- **Register interested businesses as leads** in your dashboard. Each lead is locked to you for **60 days**, so no other partner can claim it.

> **Before you register a lead:** If the dashboard says the business is already an iSuite AI customer or already being handled, you cannot claim it.

## Step 6: Bring your first client

Choose the way that fits the client.

### Option A: Direct Sale (you earn 25%)

Best when you have met the client and they are ready to start.

1. Open **Payment links** in your dashboard and choose the client (or pick them from your leads).
2. Choose the product and plan.
3. Give a discount if needed, up to 25% of the plan price. The discount comes out of your own commission.
4. Check the preview: what the client pays and what you earn.
5. Send the link to the client on WhatsApp.
6. The client starts the **free trial** using the link. Our team contacts them for setup.

### Option B: Referral (you earn 15%)

Best when the client wants to explore on their own first.

1. Copy your **referral link** from the dashboard.
2. Share it on WhatsApp, in your social posts, or as a QR code.
3. The client signs up and buys on their own at the published price.
4. You get credit if they buy within **90 days** of first clicking your link.

> **Never collect money from a client.** Every payment must go through the official iSuite AI payment link. Cash, UPI or bank transfers to you are not allowed.

**What happens next:** iSuite AI does the demo, setup, WhatsApp connection and support. You can follow the client's status in **Clients**.

## Step 7: Get paid

| Stage | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | The client is on free trial |
| Earned | The client made their first payment after the trial |
| Paid | Your commission was sent to your bank |

- **Payout day:** The 5th of every month, for all commission earned and received by iSuite AI before that date.
- **Statement:** You get a statement showing every sale, your commission and any tax deducted.
- **GST-registered partners:** Upload your GST invoice for the commission before the payout date. We pay GST on top.
- **Renewals:** Every year your client renews, you earn the same commission again, as long as you are an active partner.
