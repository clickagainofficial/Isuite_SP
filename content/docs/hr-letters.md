Admin & HR issues every letter below on company letterhead. The General Manager signs. Fill every **[square-bracket]** field, then keep a signed copy in the employee's file.

> **Before first use:** have the company's CA or lawyer check these templates once, especially the probation, notice period and confidentiality terms.

## When each letter is issued

| Letter | When | Signed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer letter | When a candidate is selected | General Manager |
| Appointment letter | On the joining day | General Manager and employee |
| Confidentiality agreement | On the joining day, before any client or partner data is shared | General Manager and employee |
| Salary revision letter | When salary changes | General Manager |
| Warning letter | After a serious or repeated problem, once it has been discussed with the employee | General Manager |
| Experience and relieving letter | On the last working day | General Manager |

## 1. Offer letter

[Company letterhead]

Date: [Date]

To: [Candidate name], [Address]

**Subject: Offer of employment as [Role]**

Dear [Candidate name],

We are happy to offer you the position of **[Role]** at [Company legal name] (iSuite AI), Madurai.

- **Joining date:** [Date]
- **Monthly salary:** ₹[Amount]
- **Working days:** Monday to Saturday
- **Shift:** 9:30 AM–6:30 PM or 10:30 AM–7:30 PM (you choose one)
- **Reports to:** General Manager

Please confirm your acceptance by [Date]. Your appointment letter will be given on your joining day.

For [Company legal name],

[Name], General Manager

## 2. Appointment letter

[Company letterhead]

Date: [Date]

To: [Employee name], [Address]

**Subject: Appointment as [Role]**

Dear [Employee name],

We are pleased to appoint you as **[Role]** at [Company legal name] (iSuite AI), from **[Joining date]**, on these terms:

1. **Role:** your work, targets and KPIs are in your role card. You report to the General Manager.
2. **Salary:** ₹[Amount] per month, paid by bank transfer on [Salary date] of each month. For days worked in November 2026, you are paid per day for the days you work.
3. **Working hours:** Monday to Saturday. Your shift is **[9:30 AM–6:30 PM / 10:30 AM–7:30 PM]**. Every working day starts with the 10:30 AM morning meeting.
4. **Place of work:** 3/501 Subash Street, Muneeswarar Nagar, Iyer Bungalow, Madurai 625014.
5. **Probation:** [3] months from your joining date.
6. **Leave:** as per the company leave policy.
7. **Notice period:** [30] days' written notice from either side. During probation, [7] days.
8. **Confidentiality:** you must sign and follow the confidentiality agreement.
9. **Company property:** laptops, phones, SIM cards, accounts and data belong to the company and must be returned on your last day.
10. **Conduct:** you follow the team rules in the Team Operating Plan. Fraud, taking money from clients or partners, or leaking data can end your employment without notice.

Please sign below to accept.

For [Company legal name],

[Name], General Manager

## 3. Confidentiality agreement

[Company letterhead]

This agreement is between [Company legal name] (iSuite AI), Madurai ("the Company") and [Employee name] ("the Employee"), dated [Date].

1. **Confidential information** means any information the Employee sees at work, including client and partner names, phone numbers, emails, KYC documents, bank details, prices, commission, business plans, software and passwords.
2. The Employee will use confidential information **only for their work** at the Company.
3. The Employee will **not share, copy, download or take** confidential information outside the Company's systems, during or after employment.
4. The Employee will **not contact** the Company's clients or partners for personal business, or for another company, using information gained at work.
5. On leaving, the Employee will **return or delete** all confidential information and company property.
6. This agreement continues after the Employee leaves the Company.
7. Breaking this agreement can lead to termination and legal action under Indian law. Courts at Madurai, Tamil Nadu have jurisdiction.

For [Company legal name]: [Name], General Manager · Signature: __________

Employee: [Employee name] · Signature: __________ · Date: __________
