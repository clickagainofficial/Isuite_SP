**Internal document. For the iSuite AI team only. Do not share with partners.**

Commission is paid once a month, on the **5th**, for all Qualified Sales whose payment has reached iSuite AI's account by then. If the 5th is a bank holiday, pay on the next working day. Start this SOP early enough that the transfer goes out on the 5th.

## At a glance

| Step | What the team does |
| --- | --- |
| 1. List | List every client payment received since the last payout |
| 2. Attribute | Confirm which partner each sale belongs to |
| 3. Calculate | Work out the commission for each sale |
| 4. Check the partner | PAN, bank account, GST invoice, partner status |
| 5. Deduct TDS | Where the law requires it |
| 6. Pay | Bank transfer on the 5th |
| 7. Report | Send the statement and update the records |

## Step 1: List the qualifying sales

Include a sale only when iSuite AI has **actually received** the client's payment.

| Include | Leave out |
| --- | --- |
| First payment after the free trial | Clients still on free trial |
| Renewals | Refunded payments |
| Upgrades (on the amount paid for the upgrade) | Sales that are not eligible (Program Rules, section 7) |
| | Sales on hold for review |

## Step 2: Confirm attribution

Each client brings one commission, paid to one partner. iSuite AI's records decide.

- A valid **Lead Registration lock** (60 days) takes priority.
- Without a lock, the **last link** the client used to buy decides.
- If a locked lead buys through another partner's link, the partner holding the lock is credited at the **Referral rate (10%)**. The other partner earns nothing on that client.
- A Referral counts only if the client bought within **90 days** of first clicking the link.

## Step 3: Calculate the commission

| Method | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Direct Sale | 15% of the Plan Price |
| Referral | 10% of the Plan Price |

- Use the Plan Price **excluding GST**.
- On a Direct Sale, subtract any discount the partner gave. The discount comes out of the partner's commission.
- Pay only on the amount iSuite AI received.

> **Example:** Clinic Growth, Plan Price ₹22,999, Direct Sale, partner gave a ₹2,000 discount. Commission = 15% × ₹22,999 = ₹3,450, minus ₹2,000 = **₹1,450**.

## Step 4: Check the partner

Before paying, confirm for each partner:

- [ ] The partner was active when the client paid
- [ ] A valid PAN is on record. No PAN, no payout.
- [ ] The bank account is the one verified in KYC
- [ ] GST-registered partner: a GST invoice for this commission has been received

**GST:** A GST-registered partner gets GST at 18% on top of the commission, against their invoice. If the invoice has not arrived, remind the partner and hold the GST amount until it does. A partner who is not GST-registered gets the commission without GST.

## Step 5: Deduct TDS

- Deduct TDS only where Indian income-tax law requires it, at the rate in force on the payment date. Confirm the rate with the company's accountant.
- Deposit the TDS against the partner's PAN.
- Show the TDS amount in the partner's statement.

## Step 6: Pay

1. Get the payout list approved.
2. Send each payout by **bank transfer** to the account verified in KYC.
3. Record the transfer reference against each payout.

## Step 7: Send the statement and update records

Send every paid partner a statement showing:

- Each sale, with the client, plan and method
- Commission earned on each sale
- TDS deducted
- GST added (if any)
- Net amount paid and the transfer date

Then mark each commission as **Paid**, so the partner sees it in their dashboard.

## Holds, refunds and exits

- **Hold:** If a sale looks like fraud or a breach of the rules, hold its commission while it is reviewed, and tell the partner the reason in writing.
- **Refund:** No commission is paid on a refunded amount. If it was already paid, adjust it in the next payout.
- **Partner has left:** Pay commission on sales the client paid for before the leaving date, on the next payout date. Do not pay on renewals after the partnership ended.
- **Removed for fraud or serious breach:** Do not pay commission on the wrongful sales.

## Disputes

A partner can raise an issue about commission or attribution in writing within **30 days** of the payout statement. Review it and reply within **15 working days**. Record the issue and the outcome.

## Payout day checklist

- [ ] Every received payment since the last payout is listed
- [ ] Trials, refunds, ineligible and held sales are left out
- [ ] Attribution checked for every sale
- [ ] Commission calculated at the right rate, minus any partner discount
- [ ] PAN and bank account confirmed for every partner being paid
- [ ] GST invoices received from GST-registered partners
- [ ] TDS deducted where required
- [ ] Payout list approved
- [ ] Transfers sent on the 5th
- [ ] Statements sent and commissions marked Paid
