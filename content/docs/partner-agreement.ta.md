Indha Agreement iSuite AI-kkum keezhe sollirukkura Solution Partner-kkum idaiye pottukkappadudhu. Idhu moolama Partner, iSuite AI-kku clients-a konduvandhu, Solution Partner Program Rules padi commission sambaadikkalaam. Partner oru individual-a irundhaalum business-a irundhaalum idhe Agreement dhaan apply aagum.

## 1. Appointment (Niyamanam)

1. iSuite AI, Partner-a oru non-exclusive Solution Partner-a niyamikkiradhu, India-la pudhu clients-kku iSuite AI products-a introduce panni vikka.
2. Indha appointment non-exclusive. iSuite AI veru partners-a yum niyamikkalaam, edha area-la yum neradiya vikkalaam.
3. Partner-kku fixed territory kidaiyaadhu, minimum sales target um kidaiyaadhu.

## 2. Program Rules

1. **iSuite AI Solution Partner Program Rules** ("Program Rules") indha Agreement-oda oru paagama irukkum. Adhu commission rates, attribution, discounts, payouts matrum nadathai (conduct) ellaam solludhu.
2. Partner, sign panra thedhi-la jaariyila irukkura Program Rules-a padichu, yethukkittadha confirm panraar.
3. iSuite AI, Program Rules-a 30 naal munnadiya Partner-kku notice kuduthu update pannalaam. Aana already sambaadicha commission-a indha updates kuraikkadhu.
4. Indha Agreement-um Program Rules-um conflict aana, indha Agreement-ku dhaan munnurimai (prevail).

## 3. Partner-oda kadamaigal (obligations)

Partner seiyya vendiyadhu:

1. iSuite AI-a honest-a, correct-a introduce pannanum, iSuite AI publish panna plans, prices matrum material-a mattum use panni.
2. Clients-a Referral Link alladhu Direct Sale moolama mattum konduvaranum, matrum prospects-a Lead Registration moolama mattum claim pannanum, Program Rules-la sollirukkura padi.
3. Client kitta irundhu edha panamum collect pannakoodaadhu. Ellaa payments-um iSuite AI-oda official payment links moolama dhaan povanum.
4. Program Rules allow pannaadha results, features, prices alladhu discounts-a edhuvum promise pannakoodaadhu.
5. Program Rules-la irukkura code of conduct-a follow pannanum, idhula spam, brand use matrum paid ads pathi irukkura rules um adangum.
6. Ellaa relevant chattangalai yum follow pannanum, idhula tax, consumer protection, information technology matrum data protection chattangal, matrum Meta-oda WhatsApp Business policies um adangum.
7. Thangaloda KYC details (Schedule A)-a correct-a vechchikkanum, edhaavadhu maathiram irundhaa 7 naalukkul iSuite AI-kku theriyapaduthanum.
8. Thangaloda sondha selavugala (travel, phone, marketing maadhiri) thaangale paathukkanum, iSuite AI ezhuthula kudukka oppukittaala thavira.

## 4. iSuite AI-oda kadamaigal (obligations)

iSuite AI seiyya vendiyadhu:

1. Partner-kku oru partner login, Referral Links, payment links matrum sales material kudukkanum.
2. Partner konduvara ellaa Client-ai yum contact panni, onboard panni, set up panni, support pannanum.
3. Ovvoru Lead Registration, Referral matrum Direct Sale-ai yum record pannanum, matrum Partner-kku avangaloda sales matrum commission-a kaamikkanum.
4. Partner-oda commission-a keezhe irukkura clause 5 padi yum Program Rules padi yum kudukkanum.

## 5. Commission and payment

1. Partner, Qualified Sales-la commission sambaadikkiraar, Program Rules-la irukkura rates-la: ippo **25%** Direct Sale-ku matrum **15%** Referral-ku, modhal payment-la matrum indha Agreement jaariyila irukkura varaikkum ovvoru Renewal-la yum.
2. Commission bank transfer moolama ovvoru maasam 5-aam thedhi, Schedule A-la irukkura bank account-ku, oru payout statement-oda kudukkappadum.
3. Indian income-tax chattam kettaa iSuite AI TDS deduct pannum. GST-la register aana Partner, commission-ku oru GST invoice raise pannanum, matrum iSuite AI adhoda mela GST kudukkum.
4. Indha Agreement-la Partner-kku commission mattum dhaan payment. Partner-kku salary, allowance alladhu veru edha benefit-um kidaiyaadhu.
5. Edha sale-layum fraud alladhu indha Agreement breach irukku-nnu iSuite AI nyaayamaa nambinaa, adha investigate panra varaikkum andha commission-a niruthi vechchikkalaam. Adhoda kaaranatha Partner-kku ezhuthula sollanum.

## 6. Relationship of the Parties

1. Partner oru independent contractor. Indha Agreement-la edhuvum Partner-a iSuite AI-oda employee, agent, franchisee, partner alladhu joint venturer aakkaadhu.
2. Partner, iSuite AI saarbaa edha contract-ai yum sign pannakoodaadhu, edha payment-ai yum accept pannakoodaadhu, edha promise-um pannakoodaadhu.
3. Partner veru business pannika free-aa irukkaar, aana adhu clause 3, 7 matrum 8-a breach pannakkoodaadhu.

## 7. Confidentiality and data protection

1. "Confidential Information"-nu sonnaa, iSuite AI, adhoda pricing, partners, Clients matrum business pathi irukkura public-ku theriyaadha thakaval, idha Partner indha Agreement sambandhama perradhu.
2. Partner, Confidential Information-a rahasiyama vechchikkanum, idha indha Agreement-a seiyya mattum use pannanum.
3. Partner, oru prospective Client-oda personal data-a andha Client-a iSuite AI-kku introduce panra oru kaariyathuku mattum, Client-oda therivoda collect panni use pannanum, matrum Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 matrum adhoda rules-a follow pannanum.
4. Partner, Confidential Information-a oru Client-a competing product-ku maathradhuku use pannakoodaadhu.
5. Indha clause, Agreement mudinjappuram 2 varushathuku thodarum.

## 8. Brand and intellectual property

1. iSuite AI name, logo, software, content matrum material-ku iSuite AI-ku dhaan sondham.
2. iSuite AI, Partner-kku oru limited, non-exclusive, revocable anumadhi kudukkiradhu, adhoda name, logo matrum material-a indha Agreement matrum Program Rules padi iSuite AI-a promote panra kaariyathuku mattum use panna.
3. Partner, "iSuite" irukkura edha domain, social media handle, business name alladhu trademark-a yum register pannakoodaadhu.
4. Indha Agreement mudinjaa, andha anumadhi thaanaaga mudinjidum.

## 9. Indemnity

Partner-oda indha Agreement breach, Clients kitta poy solradhu, Client data misuse, Client panam collect panradhu alladhu chattam meera-radhu moolama iSuite AI-ku varra edha loss, claim, penalty alladhu cost-ai yum Partner iSuite AI-ku kattanum.

## 10. Limitation of liability

1. Rendu Party-la edha Party-um marra Party-ku indirect losses-ku, udhaaranam izhandha laabam (lost profit), izhandha business alladhu reputation-ku vandha paadhippu-ku, kaasu kudukka vendaam.
2. Indha Agreement padi ellaa claims-ku-m iSuite AI Partner-ku kudukka koodiya adhigapatcha thogai, claim-ku munnaadi irukkura 12 maasathula Partner-ku kudutha motta commission mattum dhaan.
3. Chattam oru limit-a allow pannaadha idathula indha limit apply aagaadhu, matrum clause 7 matrum 9 padi Partner kattanum-na thogaiya idhu kuraikkadhu.

## 11. Term and termination

1. Indha Agreement sign panra thedhi-la aarambikkum, edhaavadhu oru Party mudikkura varaikkum thodarum.
2. Edhaavadhu oru Party, indha Agreement-la irukkura contact details-ku email alladhu WhatsApp moolama 30 naal munnadiya ezhuthula notice kuduthu indha Agreement-a mudikkalaam.
3. Partner fraud panna, poy claims panna, spam anuppina, Client panam collect panna, brand-a misuse panna alladhu indha Agreement alladhu Program Rules-a serious-a breach panna, iSuite AI udanadiyaaga ezhuthula notice kuduthu indha Agreement-a mudikkalaam.
4. Indha Agreement mudinjappuram:
    1. End date-ku munnaadi Clients kattina sales-oda commission, adutha payout date-la kudukkappadum, aana fraud alladhu seriously breach irukkura sales-oda commission thavira.
    2. Renewal commission nirkum.
    3. Partner iSuite AI name, logo matrum material use panradha niruthanum, matrum Confidential Information-a thiruppi kuduthuduvaanum alladhu delete pannanum.
    4. Clients iSuite AI-oda customers-aa thodarum.
5. Clause 7, 9, 10, 12 matrum 13 indha Agreement mudinjappuram-um thodarum.

## 12. Disputes and governing law

1. Parties, edha dispute-a yum, dispute pathi ezhuthula notice vandha 30 naalukkul mudhalla pesi theertha muyarchi pannanum.
2. Indha Agreement India-oda chattangal padi nadathappadum.
3. Clause 12.1-ku uttpattu, Madurai, Tamil Nadu-la irukkura courts-ku mattum exclusive jurisdiction irukkum.

## 13. General

1. **Entire agreement:** Indha Agreement, adhoda Schedules matrum Program Rules-oda serthu, indha vishayathula Parties-ku idaiye irukkura mozhuchana (full) agreement.
2. **Changes:** Indha Agreement-la edha maathiram-um ezhuthula irukkanum, rendu Party-um sign pannanum. Program Rules updates clause 2.3 padi nadakkum.
3. **Assignment:** Partner, iSuite AI-oda ezhuthu anumadhi illaama indha Agreement-a veru yaarukku-m transfer pannakoodaadhu.
4. **Severability:** Indha Agreement-la edha paagam-um invalid aana, meedhi baagam thodarndhu apply aagum.
5. **No waiver:** Oru samayam oru right-a enforce pannaama irundhaa, andha right-a vittukuduthadhu-nnu artham illai.
6. **Notices:** Indha Agreement-la irukkura contact details-ku email alladhu WhatsApp moolama anuppina, alladhu adhula irukkura addresses-ku post moolama anuppina, Notices valid.
7. **Copies:** Indha Agreement rendu copy-la sign pannappadudhu, ovvoru Party-ku onnu.
