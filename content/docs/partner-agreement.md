This Agreement is signed between iSuite AI and the Solution Partner named below. It lets the Partner bring clients to iSuite AI and earn commission under the Solution Partner Program Rules. The same Agreement applies whether the Partner is an individual or a business.

**And:**

The person or business named in Schedule A (**"the Partner"**).

iSuite AI and the Partner are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

## 1. Appointment

1. iSuite AI appoints the Partner as a non-exclusive Solution Partner to introduce and sell iSuite AI products to new clients in India.
2. The appointment is non-exclusive. iSuite AI may appoint other partners and sell directly in any area.
3. The Partner has no fixed territory and no minimum sales target.

## 2. Program Rules

1. The **iSuite AI Solution Partner Program Rules** ("Program Rules") form part of this Agreement. They set out commission rates, attribution, discounts, payouts and conduct.
2. The Partner confirms that they have read and accept the Program Rules in force on the date of signing.
3. iSuite AI may update the Program Rules with 30 days' notice to the Partner. Updates do not reduce commission already earned.
4. If this Agreement and the Program Rules conflict, this Agreement prevails.

## 3. Partner's obligations

The Partner shall:

1. Introduce iSuite AI honestly and accurately, using only plans, prices and material published by iSuite AI.
2. Bring Clients only through a Referral Link or a Direct Sale, and claim prospects only through Lead Registration, as set out in the Program Rules.
3. Never collect any money from a Client. All payments go to iSuite AI through its official payment links.
4. Never promise results, features, prices or discounts that the Program Rules do not allow.
5. Follow the code of conduct in the Program Rules, including the rules on spam, brand use and paid ads.
6. Comply with all applicable laws, including tax, consumer protection, information technology and data protection laws, and Meta's WhatsApp Business policies.
7. Keep their KYC details (Schedule A) correct and inform iSuite AI of any change within 7 days.
8. Bear their own expenses, such as travel, phone and marketing, unless iSuite AI agrees in writing to pay them.

## 4. iSuite AI's obligations

iSuite AI shall:

1. Give the Partner a partner login, Referral Links, payment links and sales material.
2. Contact, onboard, set up and support every Client the Partner brings.
3. Record every Lead Registration, Referral and Direct Sale, and show the Partner their sales and commission.
4. Pay the Partner's commission as explained in clause 5 below and in the Program Rules.

## 5. Commission and payment

1. The Partner earns commission on Qualified Sales at the rates in the Program Rules: currently **25%** for a Direct Sale and **15%** for a Referral, on the first payment and on every Renewal while this Agreement is in force.
2. Commission is paid by bank transfer on the 5th of every month, to the bank account in Schedule A, with a payout statement.
3. iSuite AI deducts TDS where Indian income-tax law requires it. A GST-registered Partner shall raise a GST invoice for the commission, and iSuite AI shall pay GST on top.
4. Commission is the Partner's only payment under this Agreement. The Partner is not entitled to salary, allowance or any other benefit.
5. iSuite AI may hold back commission on any sale it reasonably believes involves fraud or a breach of this Agreement, while it investigates. It shall tell the Partner the reason in writing.

## 6. Relationship of the Parties

1. The Partner is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement makes the Partner an employee, agent, franchisee, partner or joint venturer of iSuite AI.
2. The Partner cannot sign any contract, accept any payment or make any promise on behalf of iSuite AI.
3. The Partner is free to carry on other business, provided it does not breach clauses 3, 7 and 8.

## 7. Confidentiality and data protection

1. "Confidential Information" means non-public information about iSuite AI, its pricing, partners, Clients and business, received by the Partner in connection with this Agreement.
2. The Partner shall keep Confidential Information secret and use it only to perform this Agreement.
3. The Partner shall collect and use a prospective Client's personal data only to introduce that Client to iSuite AI, with the Client's knowledge, and shall comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules.
4. The Partner shall not use Confidential Information to move a Client to a competing product.
5. This clause continues for 2 years after this Agreement ends.

## 8. Brand and intellectual property

1. iSuite AI owns the iSuite AI name, logo, software, content and material.
2. iSuite AI gives the Partner a limited, non-exclusive, revocable permission to use its name, logo and material only to promote iSuite AI under this Agreement and the Program Rules.
3. The Partner shall not register any domain, social media handle, business name or trademark containing "iSuite".
4. The permission ends automatically when this Agreement ends.

## 9. Indemnity

The Partner shall pay iSuite AI for any loss, claim, penalty or cost that iSuite AI suffers because of the Partner's breach of this Agreement, false statements to Clients, misuse of Client data, collection of Client money or violation of law.

## 10. Limitation of liability

1. Neither Party has to pay the other for indirect losses, such as lost profit, lost business or damage to reputation.
2. The most iSuite AI will pay the Partner for all claims under this Agreement is the total commission paid to the Partner in the 12 months before the claim.
3. This limit does not apply where the law does not allow a limit, and it does not reduce what the Partner must pay under clauses 7 and 9.

## 11. Term and termination

1. This Agreement starts on the date it is signed and continues until either Party ends it.
2. Either Party may end this Agreement by giving 30 days' written notice by email or WhatsApp to the contact details in this Agreement.
3. iSuite AI may end this Agreement immediately by written notice if the Partner commits fraud, makes false claims, sends spam, collects Client money, misuses the brand or seriously breaches this Agreement or the Program Rules.
4. When this Agreement ends:
    1. Commission on sales paid by Clients before the end date is paid on the next payout date, except commission on sales involving fraud or serious breach.
    2. Renewal commission stops.
    3. The Partner stops using the iSuite AI name, logo and material, and returns or deletes Confidential Information.
    4. Clients remain customers of iSuite AI.
5. Clauses 7, 9, 10, 12 and 13 continue after this Agreement ends.

## 12. Disputes and governing law

1. The Parties shall first try to settle any dispute by discussion within 30 days of written notice of the dispute.
2. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.
3. Subject to clause 12.1, the courts at Madurai, Tamil Nadu have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 13. General

1. **Entire agreement:** This Agreement, with its Schedules and the Program Rules, is the full agreement between the Parties on this subject.
2. **Changes:** Any change to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both Parties. Program Rules updates follow clause 2.3.
3. **Assignment:** The Partner cannot transfer this Agreement to anyone else without iSuite AI's written consent.
4. **Severability:** If any part of this Agreement is invalid, the rest continues to apply.
5. **No waiver:** Not enforcing a right at one time does not give up that right.
6. **Notices:** Notices are valid when sent by email or WhatsApp to the contact details in this Agreement, or by post to the addresses in it.
7. **Copies:** This Agreement is signed in two copies, one for each Party.
