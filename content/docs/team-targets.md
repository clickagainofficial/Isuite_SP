We start from zero: no Solution Partners, no paying customers and no Meta ads yet. The main goal is Solution Partners who sell. Direct customers come only from Meta ads at first.

## The targets

| Target | Jan 2027 | Feb 2027 | Mar 2027 | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New SPs signed | 5 | 10 | 15 | **30** |
| Direct paying customers | 5 | 8 | 12 | **25** |
| Clients retained | | | | **70%** |

- **New SP signed:** KYC verified and Partner Agreement signed by both sides (see the Partner Onboarding SOP).
- **Direct paying customer:** came through our own Meta ads and made the first payment after the free trial.
- **25 direct customers** is the fixed total. The 5 / 8 / 12 monthly split is a starting point.
- **70% retained:** 70% of clients stay with iSuite AI. Plans are yearly, so the real test comes at renewal. Until then, Customer Support tracks active clients every month.

## Phases

| Phase | Dates | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recruit | 15–30 Nov 2026 | Hire the 5 roles. Admin & HR first |
| Train | 1–31 Dec 2026 | Role training with tests. Ads, demo videos and SOPs ready. Test ads in the last week |
| Month 1 · Launch | Jan 2027 | Ads live in both funnels. Pongal campaign. Cost-per-lead check on 18 Jan |
| Month 2 · Scale | Feb 2027 | More spend on the ads that work |
| Month 3 · Optimise | Mar 2027 | Cut weak ads, push every signed SP to a first sale |

## Numbers we watch

These estimates assume about ₹300 per lead in both funnels. January shows the real cost.

| Number | Each month | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SP leads from ₹30,000 of Meta ads | About 100 | Enough to sign 5 to 15 new SPs a month |
| Direct free trials from ₹20,000 of Meta ads | About 65 | The pool the 25 direct customers come from |
| Trial → paid | About 13% on average | 25 customers from about 195 trials |
| Active SPs (signed and made at least 1 sale) | 1 / 4 / 10 by end of Jan / Feb / Mar | A signed SP who never sells does not help |
| SP lead first call | Within 1 hour | Fast calls sign more partners |

## Daily KPI board

Admin & HR updates the KPI board every working day at 6:00 PM from everyone's update.

| Column | Sent by |
| --- | --- |
| SP leads today, ad spend today (SP funnel) | Marketing |
| Direct trials today, ad spend today (Direct funnel) | Marketing |
| SP calls made, SPs signed this month | Partner Relations |
| SPs who made a first sale this month | Partner Relations |
| Setups done, calls made, trials converted this month | Customer Support |
| Expenses today | Admin & HR |

## Weekly review

Every Friday at 5:00 PM the General Manager checks:

1. Each number against this month's target.
2. Ad spend and cost per lead in both funnels, and where to move money next week.
3. Who needs help. Anyone below 70% of their target gets refresher training from the Training Head the next week.

## Resetting targets

On **Monday 18 January 2027**, after 2 weeks of ads, the General Manager checks the real cost per lead in both funnels and resets the February and March numbers. The totals (30 SPs, 25 direct customers) stay unless the General Manager changes them.
