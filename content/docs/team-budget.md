₹2,50,000 a month is the **ceiling**, not a target. Spend only what each line needs. The plan comes to **₹2,50,000 a month**, exactly the ceiling. Any new cost needs a cut somewhere else first, approved by the General Manager.

## Monthly budget

| Item | ₹/month |
| --- | --- |
| General Manager | 50,000 |
| Admin & HR | 18,000 |
| Training Head | 30,000 |
| Marketing Executive | 15,000 |
| Partner Relations | 15,000 |
| Customer Support | 15,000 |
| **Salaries total** | **1,43,000** |
| Meta ads: SP recruitment | 30,000 |
| Meta ads: Direct free trials | 20,000 |
| **Meta ads total** | **50,000** |
| **Tools** | **39,000** |
| Team event (turf, movie or games) | 8,000 |
| Top Performer reward | 3,000 |
| PR article | 7,000 |
| **Team and PR total** | **18,000** |
| **Grand total** | **2,50,000** |

## Where the money goes

| Part | ₹/month | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salaries | 1,43,000 | 57% |
| Meta ads | 50,000 | 20% |
| Tools | 39,000 | 16% |
| Team and PR | 18,000 | 7% |
| **Total** | **2,50,000** | **100%** |

## Salaries

- **Regular salary starts in December 2026.**
- Anyone who joins in November is paid **per day** for the days they work in November.
- Each person's salary, shift and working days are written in their appointment letter (see HR Letters).

## Meta ads

| Funnel | ₹/month | Who we reach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SP recruitment | 30,000 | CAs and tax consultants, Tally and software resellers, digital marketing agencies, industry suppliers, business consultants |
| Direct free trials | 20,000 | Clinics, gyms, salons, coaching centres, turfs, builders, field service and AMC companies |

60% of the ad money goes to SP recruitment, because Solution Partners are the main goal. In December only small test ads run, from 21 December.

## Tools

₹39,000 a month for the software the team runs on, such as the WhatsApp Business API, email marketing, video editing and design tools. Admin & HR keeps a list of every paid tool with its monthly cost and renewal date.

## Spending rules

- Admin & HR records every expense the same day, with the bill.
- Every expense needs the General Manager's approval.
- On the last working day of the month, Admin & HR and the General Manager compare actual spend with this budget.
