## The rhythm

| When | What | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every working day, 10:30 AM | Morning meeting + pledge, 15 minutes | Admin & HR runs it, everyone attends |
| Every working day, 6:00 PM | KPI update | Everyone sends numbers, Admin & HR updates the board |
| Monday, 10:45 AM | Weekly planning | GM + team |
| Wednesday, 4:00 PM (from January) | SP webinar | Partner Relations |
| Friday, 5:00 PM | December: weekly training test. From January: weekly review + ad spend check | Training Head / GM |
| Saturday, 11:00 AM | Video batch shoot | Marketing |
| 1st working day (from January) | Monthly targets set | GM |
| Working day before the 5th (from February) | SP payout list approved | GM |
| 5th, or next working day (from February) | SP commission payout | Admin & HR |
| 2nd Thursday (from January) | PR article release | Marketing |
| 3rd Saturday | Team event: turf, movie or games. January's event is the office Pongal celebration on 14 Jan | Admin & HR |
| Last working day (from January) | Top Performer award + budget review | Admin & HR, GM |

The first SP payout is on 5 February 2027, for commission earned in January.

## Holidays

Festival dates follow the Tamil Nadu 2027 holiday list. The calendar treats **Christmas (25 Dec)**, **Pongal (15 Jan)**, **Thiruvalluvar Day (16 Jan)** and **Republic Day (26 Jan)** as office holidays. 1 January is a working day because it is launch day. The General Manager confirms the final holiday list, and Admin & HR adds any other holidays.

## Changing the calendar

The dates, times and owners in the calendar come from `lib/team-plan.ts`. Change them there and the calendar updates.
