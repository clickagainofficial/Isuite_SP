Marketing brings leads into both funnels: businesses that can become Solution Partners, and businesses that start a free trial on their own. The Marketing Executive does the day-to-day work. **The General Manager leads Marketing directly:** sets the plan and the ad budget, and approves every ad, video and PR article before it goes out.

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Reports to | General Manager |
| Hands leads to | Partner Relations (SP leads), Customer Support (free-trial users) |
| Budget | Meta ads ₹50,000 a month: SP ₹30,000, Direct ₹20,000 |

## Who does what in Marketing

| Work | Marketing Executive | General Manager |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meta ads | Sets up campaigns, makes creatives, checks cost per lead every day | Sets the budget, approves new ads, moves money between ads on Friday |
| Daily video | Plans, shoots, edits and posts | Approves the week's videos, appears in videos when needed |
| PR article | Writes the draft, gets it published, shares it on social media | Approves before release |
| SP demo videos and customer guides | Makes them | Approves them |
| WhatsApp and email campaigns | Prepares and sends | Approves the message |

## Responsibilities

### Meta ads

- **SP recruitment funnel, ₹30,000 a month:** CAs and tax consultants, Tally and software resellers, digital marketing agencies, industry suppliers and business consultants.
- **Direct free-trial funnel, ₹20,000 a month:** clinics, gyms, salons, coaching centres, turfs, builders, field service and AMC companies.
- Check cost per lead every day. Bring the numbers to the Friday review, where the GM decides where the money moves.
- Hand every SP lead to Partner Relations and every free-trial user to Customer Support the same day.
- Pongal campaign in January.

### Content

- **Daily video** for social media, Monday to Saturday. Shoot in batches on Saturday at 11:00 AM.
- **1 PR article a month**, released on the 2nd Thursday.
- **Demo videos for Solution Partners,** one per product: Turf, Gym, Salon, Field Service & AMC, Clinic, Coaching and Real Estate. iSuite AI does not give live sales demos, so partners use these videos.
- **How-to guides for customers,** one set per product. Customer Support uses them during setup.

### WhatsApp and email marketing

- Only to contacts who opted in.
- Only approved WhatsApp templates. No bought contact lists.

## Daily, weekly, monthly

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Every working day, 10:30 AM | Morning meeting |
| Every working day | Check ad spend and cost per lead, post the day's video |
| Every working day, 6:00 PM | Send SP leads, direct trials and ad spend for the KPI board |
| Friday, 5:00 PM | Weekly review + ad spend check with the GM |
| Saturday, 11:00 AM | Video batch shoot |
| 2nd Thursday | PR article release |

## December

- Product and role training with the Training Head in the first 2 weeks.
- Set up the Meta ad accounts and make the ad creatives for both funnels. The GM approves them.
- Make the 7 SP demo videos by 18 December.
- Run small test ads from 21 December. Ads go fully live on 1 January 2027.
