Quick answers to common questions. The **Solution Partner Program Rules** and the **Solution Partner Agreement** are the full and final word. If an answer here ever differs from them, they apply.

## Joining

### Who can become a Solution Partner?

Any business registered in India. You do not need a GST number to join.

### Is there a joining fee or deposit?

No. Joining is free.

### What documents do I need?

Your PAN and proof of your bank account (a cancelled cheque or the front page of your passbook). If you are GST-registered, your GST certificate too. If you join as a firm, LLP or company, proof that the person signing can sign for the business.

### How long does approval take?

We review applications within about 2 working days. After that you complete KYC and sign the Solution Partner Agreement, and then your partner login is created.

### Is there a sales target or a fixed area?

No. There is no minimum target and no fixed territory. You can bring clients from anywhere in India. The partnership is non-exclusive, so other partners and iSuite AI's own team can also sell in the same area.

### Am I an employee of iSuite AI?

No. You are an independent partner. You earn commission only, with no salary or allowance, and you are free to run your other business alongside.

## Earning

### How much do I earn?

| How the client buys | Your commission |
| --- | --- |
| Direct Sale: you send the iSuite AI payment link | 15% of the Plan Price |
| Referral: the client buys through your referral link | 10% of the Plan Price |

Commission is calculated on the plan price excluding GST.

### Do I earn when the client renews?

Yes. You earn the same rate on every renewal, for as long as the client renews and you remain an active partner.

### What if the client upgrades to a bigger plan?

You earn the same rate on the amount the client pays for the upgrade.

### The client is on a free trial. When do I earn?

When the client makes their first payment after the trial ends. If the client leaves during the trial without paying, there is no commission.

### Can I give the client a discount?

Yes, on a Direct Sale only. You can give any discount up to 15% of the plan price, and it comes out of your own commission. You can also give it on a renewal. There is no discount on Referral sales.

> **Example:** Plan price ₹22,999. Your commission is ₹3,450. If you give a ₹2,000 discount, the client pays ₹20,999 + GST and you earn ₹1,450.

### Can I sell every product and plan?

Yes. You can create a payment link for any iSuite AI product and plan. Check what each plan includes before you promise a feature. For example, some Basic plans do not include the AI agent.

### Can I earn commission on my own business?

No. If you buy iSuite AI for your own business, or for a business you own, run or work in, no commission is paid.

## Leads and clients

### What is Lead Registration?

It is how you claim a business you are talking to. Once you register a lead, it is locked to you for 60 days and no other partner can register it. Registering a lead does not pay commission by itself. You earn when that business buys.

### Do I have to register a lead before selling?

No. It is optional, but it protects you while the client is still deciding.

### What happens after 60 days?

If the business has not bought, the lock ends and the lead is free again. You cannot register the same lead again for the next 30 days, but you can still bring that business through a Direct Sale or a Referral.

### Why was my lead rejected?

The business is already an iSuite AI customer, is already being handled by iSuite AI's own team, or is already registered by another partner.

### How long does my referral link stay valid?

If a business buys within 90 days of first clicking your link, you get the credit.

### Does the payment link expire?

Yes. Every payment link has an expiry date that is set when the link is created. After that date it stops working, and you can send a new one.

### Another partner is talking to the same client. Who gets the commission?

- If one of you has registered the client as a lead and the lock is active, that partner gets the credit.
- If nobody has a lock, the partner whose link the client actually used to buy gets the credit.
- If a locked lead buys through a different partner's link, the partner holding the lock earns the Referral rate (10%).

One client always means one commission, to one partner.

### Who explains the product, and who sets up the account?

You explain the product and convince the client. iSuite AI does not give sales demos, because convincing the client is your part. Once the client pays, iSuite AI sets up the account, guides the client and gives all support.

### Can I collect the payment from the client?

Never. Every payment must go through the official iSuite AI payment link. Taking cash, UPI or a bank transfer from a client is a serious breach and can end your partnership immediately.

### What if the client cancels or does not renew?

You keep the commission already earned on payments the client made. You simply do not earn on renewals that do not happen.

### What if the client gets a refund?

Payments are not refunded once a plan period starts. In the rare cases where iSuite AI's refund policy allows one, such as a double charge, no commission is paid on the refunded amount.

## Payouts and tax

### When do I get paid?

On the 5th of every month, by bank transfer, for all commission on payments that have reached iSuite AI by then. If the 5th is a bank holiday, it is paid on the next working day.

### Is there a minimum payout amount?

No.

### How do I know what I was paid for?

Every payout comes with a statement that lists each sale, the commission, any tax deducted and the amount paid. You can also see it in your dashboard.

### Is TDS deducted from my commission?

Only where Indian income-tax law requires it, at the rate the law sets. Any TDS deducted is deposited against your PAN and shown in your statement, so you can claim it in your income-tax return.

### Why is PAN compulsory?

Payouts cannot be released without a valid PAN.

### I am GST-registered. What changes?

You send a GST invoice for your commission and iSuite AI pays 18% GST on top. If you are not GST-registered, you receive the commission without GST.

### Can I change my bank account?

Yes. Tell us within 7 days of any change to your details. Payouts go to the new account only after it is verified.

## Rules and conduct

### What am I not allowed to do?

- Promise results the product does not guarantee, or features it does not have.
- Send bulk unsolicited WhatsApp messages, SMS, emails or calls, or buy contact lists.
- Create websites, social pages or domain names with "iSuite" in them, or run ads on the words "iSuite" or "iSuite AI" without written approval.
- Introduce yourself as an iSuite AI employee. Say "iSuite AI Solution Partner".
- Share client details or iSuite AI's confidential information.

### Can iSuite AI change the commission or the rules?

Yes, with 30 days' notice to partners. Changes do not reduce commission you have already earned.

### How do I leave the program?

Give 30 days' written notice by email or WhatsApp. Commission on sales paid for before your leaving date is still paid on the next payout date. Renewal commission stops once the partnership ends, and your clients remain iSuite AI customers.

### Can iSuite AI remove me?

Yes, with 30 days' notice, or immediately for fraud, false claims, spam, collecting client money, brand misuse or a serious breach of the rules.

### I think my commission is wrong. What do I do?

Write to us within 30 days of the payout statement. We review it and reply within 15 working days.
